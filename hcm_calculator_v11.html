Terminology & Formulae
RHC — Residual Hazard Cloud (HazPoC/shift) = S × P × F × E × T ÷ 8  |  PHC — Potential Hazard Cloud = RHC₁ ÷ (1 − SRS₁) — the uncontrolled baseline, fixed from current condition  |  RHC₂ — Residual Hazard Cloud, Future = PHC × (1 − SRS₂)  |  AHC — Acceptable Hazard Cloud = S × P × FALARP × E × T ÷ 8 — the tolerable level per ALARP target frequency  |  UCHC — Uncontrolled HazPoC Above AHC = max(RHC − AHC, 0) — excess hazard still above the acceptable threshold  |  SRS — Safety Reliability Score = 1 − ∏(1 − rᵢ) — combined probability that at least one barrier prevents escalation  |  Sp — Safety Performance = 1 − RHC ÷ PHC — fraction of potential hazard controlled (0–100%)  |  SpALARP — minimum Sp required to meet the ALARP frequency target  |  Gap closure rate = (Sp₂ − Sp₁) ÷ (SpALARP − Sp₁)  |  Bike SRS = MIN(SRSPed, SRSVeh) × 0.9 by default — or enter r manually per barrier using the "manual ✎" toggle
