Framework

The HCM / ALARP
Methodology

The Hazard Cloud Model is a proprietary quantitative safety framework developed by Dr. Mohammad Saeed Torabi — structured within the internationally recognised ALARP principle.

01 — IDENTIFY

Hazard Identification

Systematic cataloguing of hazard categories at the crossing or facility. Each hazard is characterised by type, exposed group, and operational frequency.

02 — QUANTIFY

Risk Quantification

Severity × Probability × Frequency parameters establish a baseline Hazard Score (HS) for each hazard category using the HCM formula.

03 — BARRIER

Safety Barrier Assessment

Each active barrier is evaluated for condition, layer type (L1–L4), and effectiveness. Residual risk is calculated after barrier portfolio application.

04 — DEMONSTRATE

ALARP Demonstration

Safety Performance (SP) is benchmarked against the ALARP threshold. Status is classified as Demonstrated, In Margin, or Needs Enhancement.

ALARP Performance Zones
Intolerable
Risk cannot be justified. Immediate action required. Barriers inadequate or absent.
ALARP / Tolerable
Risk tolerable only if further reduction is impracticable. Continuous improvement required.
Broadly Acceptable
Safety demonstrated. Risk reduced ALARP. Maintain barrier performance and audit schedule.
